## Where the words come from

The README is a shorter version of his portfolio at **https://fernwire.co.za**. The portfolio's source is the private repo `yushirm/fernwire-site`, which is authoritative: its `public/index.html` is what's published and `docs/copy.md` is a readable mirror. When the portfolio changes, this README follows in a matching PR (fernwire-site decision D91).

- Keep the same section order as the site: Things I've built, How I test, What I do for a living, Apps for home, For fun, Say hello.
- Use the site's approved wording, trimmed. Don't write new claims here that aren't on the site.
- No screenshots of the private apps here. Point to the portfolio for those.

## Rules

1. **Honest content only.** Every line must be true and on the site. No invented stats, badges, skill bars or "expert" claims.
2. **Never name his employer, its clients or past employers, and give no job dates.** Industries and tools are fine ("online gaming platforms", "a digital banking platform").
3. **Link only public things.** Check that a repo is public, logged out, before linking it.
4. **Voice:** plain, short sentences, British/SA spelling. No emoji bullets, no hype ("10x", "rockstar", "passionate about", "seamless"), no "not X, but Y" lines, no exclamation-mark enthusiasm.
5. **Colander** says "Not affiliated with Takealot" and makes no claim that could hurt Takealot.
6. **Never push to `main`.** Open a PR, link it to the fernwire-site PR it follows, and leave the merge to Yushir.
